Data & Privacy

The short version: your data, our discretion, your control.

OSEA is a small nonprofit. We don't sell data, we don't run ads, and we don't track visitors beyond what's necessary to keep the platform running and accurate. The full policy below.

§ I What we collect

Photographs you submit, together with whatever EXIF metadata they carry (date, GPS, camera model, dimensions). We don't strip metadata before processing because the time and place are scientific data.

Information you type into the form — your name and email (both optional), location notes, depth, conditions, and any free-text observations.

A salted hash of your IP address, kept temporarily for rate-limiting and abuse prevention. This is not used for tracking and is rotated periodically.

Aggregate analytics on which pages get traffic. We use a privacy-respecting analytics service that does not set tracking cookies and does not share data with third parties.

§ II What we do with it

Research only. Photographs are catalogued, identified, and used to build long-term records of individual marine animals. The research-use consent is non-negotiable; without it we can't accept the submission.

Public display only with consent. Approved photographs are shown on public pages — gallery, partner pages, outreach materials — only when you've checked the public-display consent. The research-use checkbox alone does not authorize public display.

Submitter attribution only with a name. If you provided a name and ticked public-display consent, we credit you. If you didn't provide a name, the photograph is published anonymously.

Email is for correspondence only. If you provided an email, we use it to confirm your submission, send a moderation result, or follow up if a scientist has a question. We don't subscribe you to anything; we don't share it.

§ III Anti-poaching: GPS scrubbing

Sevengill sharks (and several other species we work with) face poaching pressure. Precise location data on individual animals is genuinely dangerous if leaked.

On any public-facing page, GPS coordinates are rounded to the nearest 0.1° (~ 11 km grid). Site visitors and partners see only the regional fix.

OSEA scientists and approved moderators see precise coordinates internally for the purpose of analysis and field follow-up. Precise coordinates are never exported to partner organizations except under formal data-sharing agreements with appropriate use restrictions.

§ IV Right to delete

You can request deletion of any submission you made. We honour these requests within 30 days. The photograph is removed from disk; the database record is retained as a tombstone (no submitter info, no photograph, no precise GPS) so the encounter and individual records stay consistent.

Email deletion requests to privacy@oseascience.com with the field-note number(s) you'd like removed. If you didn't keep the number, your email address (if you provided it) is sufficient to identify the submissions.

§ V Photo licensing

By submitting a photograph, you confirm that you own it (or have permission to share it) and grant OSEA a non-exclusive, irrevocable license to use it for non-commercial scientific research. You retain copyright and all other rights to the photograph.

Public-display consent (separate from research-use consent) is what authorizes display of your photograph on the public catalogue, partner pages, and outreach materials. You can withdraw public-display consent at any time without affecting the research-use license.

§ VI Data retention & security

Approved photographs are kept indefinitely (this is a long-term catalogue). Rejected submissions are kept in a 30-day audit buffer then permanently deleted. Submission attempts (rate-limit table) are kept for 90 days.

The platform runs on industry-standard hosting with SSL on all endpoints. We don't store payment information — donations are processed by Stripe, who handle their own security. We don't run authenticated user accounts for submitters in V1; partner-organization accounts (when applicable) use passwordless email login.

§ VII Questions

Email privacy@oseascience.com for anything not covered above. Policy last updated on the date this page was last edited; previous versions are visible in the platform's git history.
