Privacy & Data Practices

Your data, our discretion, your control.

OSEA is a California-based nonprofit. We collect only what we need to run a scientific catalogue. We don't sell or share personal information for advertising. The full policy is below; California-specific rights are in § VIII.

Effective date: last updated when this page was last edited; previous versions are visible in the project's public git history.

§ I Who this policy applies to

This policy covers visitors to OSEA's citizen-science platform (currently hosted at dbold23.github.io/osea-citizen-platform and intended to migrate to sightings.oseascience.com). It applies to anyone who submits a photograph, registers as a partner organization, donates, or simply browses.

It does not cover the main OSEA website at oseascience.com, which has its own privacy notice maintained by Wix.

§ II What we collect

We collect the following categories of personal information, only when you provide them:

Photographs you submit, together with whatever EXIF metadata they carry (date, GPS coordinates, camera model, image dimensions). We do not strip metadata before processing because the time and place of a sighting are scientific data.

Information you type into a form: optional name and email address, location notes, depth, conditions, and any free-text observations.

Partner-organization application data if you apply to become a partner: organization name, type, region, contact name and email, role, and a free-text description.

Donation records processed by Stripe. We receive from Stripe only the donor's name, email (if provided), donation amount, and tier. We do not store, see, or have access to your payment-card details.

A salted hash of your IP address, retained temporarily for rate-limiting and abuse prevention. The hash is rotated periodically and is not used for tracking, profiling, or advertising.

Aggregated, non-identifying analytics on which pages receive traffic. We use a privacy-respecting analytics provider that does not set tracking cookies and does not share data with third parties for advertising.

What we do not collect: precise location of visitors (other than the GPS embedded in submitted photographs), browsing history outside this site, government-issued identifiers, financial account information, biometric identifiers, racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, sexual orientation, or health information.

§ III Why we collect it (purposes)

Scientific research. Submitted photographs are catalogued, identified using computer-vision models, and used to build long-term records of individual marine animals. The research-use consent is required for us to accept a submission; without it we cannot process the photograph.

Public outreach, only with separate explicit consent (the public-display checkbox). The research-use consent alone does not authorize public display of your photograph.

Partner-organization administration: reviewing and approving applications, generating partner pages and QR codes, and providing quarterly impact reports.

Donor recognition and acknowledgment: issuing tax acknowledgments (see donation page), delivering tier benefits, and listing donors in the colophon and contributor wall, only when donors opt in to public recognition.

Operational security: rate-limiting, spam prevention, and abuse moderation via the IP hash and submission attempt log.

§ IV How long we keep it

Approved photographs and their associated metadata are kept indefinitely. The catalogue's value is its longitudinal record; this is a long-term scientific archive.

Rejected submissions are kept in a 30-day audit buffer (so a moderator can reverse a mistake), then permanently deleted from disk.

Submission attempt logs (IP hash table) are kept for 90 days for abuse prevention, then deleted.

Partner-organization records are kept while the partnership is active and for 7 years after termination, to satisfy nonprofit recordkeeping requirements.

Donor records are kept for 7 years to satisfy IRS recordkeeping requirements for 501(c)(3) organizations.

Soft-deleted records (those a submitter has asked us to remove) retain only a tombstone row with no submitter information, no photograph, and no precise GPS, so the encounter and individual records stay consistent.

§ V Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We share information only with the following categories of service providers, all bound by contracts limiting their use of the data:

Stripe, our payment processor, for donation transactions. Stripe's privacy policy governs their handling.

GitHub, our static frontend host (GitHub Pages).

Render (or equivalent backend host, when deployed), for the API server that processes submissions.

Mailgun (planned for V2), for inbound email submission processing and outbound moderation-decision emails.

Authorized scientific partners, only under formal written data-sharing agreements with appropriate use restrictions, for the purposes of marine-biology research. Photographs displayed in any external context show only fuzzed GPS (rounded to 0.1°, roughly 11 km) unless the submitter has affirmatively granted both research-use and public-display consent and we have a specific reason to share precise coordinates.

Government authorities, only when required by valid legal process and only to the minimum extent required.

§ VI Anti-poaching: GPS scrubbing

Sevengill sharks (and several other species we work with) face poaching pressure. Precise location data on individual animals can be genuinely dangerous if leaked.

On any public-facing page, GPS coordinates are rounded to the nearest 0.1° (roughly 11 km grid). Site visitors and partner-organization staff see only the regional fix.

OSEA scientists and approved moderators see precise coordinates internally for analysis and field follow-up. Precise coordinates are never exported to partner organizations except under formal written data-sharing agreements with appropriate use restrictions.

§ VII Photo licensing

By submitting a photograph, you confirm that you own it (or have permission to share it) and grant OSEA a non-exclusive, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free license to use it for non-commercial scientific research and, only if you check the public-display consent, for public outreach. You retain copyright and all other rights to the photograph.

Public-display consent (separate from research-use consent) is what authorizes display of your photograph on the public catalogue, partner pages, gallery, social media, and outreach materials. You can withdraw public-display consent at any time, after which we will remove the photograph from public surfaces, while retaining it in the research catalogue.

§ VIII Your California rights (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018 as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020 gives you the following rights with respect to your personal information. You may exercise any of these rights by emailing example@oseascience.com (placeholder; we will publish a real address before public launch).

Right to know. You may request that we disclose what categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the categories of sources, the business or commercial purposes for collecting or sharing it, and the categories of third parties with which we share it.

Right to delete. You may request deletion of any personal information we have collected from you. We will honour such requests within 45 days, with a possible 45-day extension if necessary. Photograph files are removed from disk; the database record is retained as a tombstone with no submitter information, no photograph, and no precise GPS, so the underlying encounter and individual records stay consistent.

Right to correct. You may request correction of inaccurate personal information we have collected.

Right to opt out of "selling" or "sharing." We do not sell personal information for monetary or other valuable consideration, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. There is therefore nothing to opt out of, but you have the right and we honour it as a matter of policy.

Right to limit use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by CPRA.

Right to non-discrimination. We will not deny you services, charge you a different price, or provide a lower quality of service because you exercised any of these rights.

Authorized agents. You may designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf. We will require written proof of authorization and may verify your identity directly with you.

Verification. To protect against fraudulent requests, we may ask you to verify your identity before fulfilling a request, by matching information you provide to information we already have associated with the submissions in question (typically the submission ID and the email used at submission time).

Response time. We will confirm receipt of your request within 10 business days and substantively respond within 45 calendar days, with the possibility of one 45-day extension if reasonably necessary, with notice.

Appeals. If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our denial email. We will respond to appeals within 60 days. If you are unsatisfied with our response, you may contact the California Attorney General at oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa.

§ IX Other state and international rights

Residents of other US states with comprehensive privacy laws (Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, Virginia, and others as enacted) have substantively similar rights, which we honour as a matter of policy.

Residents of the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and similar jurisdictions have rights under the GDPR / UK GDPR including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. We honour these rights upon verified request to the same address.

§ X Children

The platform is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent (COPPA). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has submitted personal information without your consent, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

If you are between 13 and 16 and a California resident, the "shine the light" provisions of CCPA require us to obtain affirmative authorization before "selling" or "sharing" your personal information. We do neither, regardless.

§ XI Security

The platform runs on industry-standard hosting with HTTPS on all endpoints. Donations are processed by Stripe; we do not store, access, or have visibility into payment-card data. The submission database is hosted on a managed cloud provider with at-rest encryption.

Despite reasonable safeguards, no system is perfectly secure. If we ever discover a security incident affecting personal information, we will notify affected users and (where required) regulators in accordance with applicable law.

§ XII Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the platform evolves or as the law requires. Material changes will be flagged on this page; the full revision history is publicly visible in the project's git repository. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent substantive update.

§ XIII Contact

Email example@oseascience.com for any question, request, or appeal under this policy. The address shown is a placeholder and will be replaced with a real privacy-rights inbox before public launch; until then, please reach out via the main OSEA site.
