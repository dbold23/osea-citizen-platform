About

A small nonprofit doing large-catalogue work.

OSEA, the Ocean Science Expedition Academy, exists to build the world's most useful photographic catalogues of patterned marine animals. We're scientists, divers, and software engineers; we share data; we don't sell anything.

§ I What we do

Marine animals with unique patterns, sevengill sharks, leopard sharks, whale sharks, eagle rays, manta rays, harbour seals, can be identified from photographs. Track those individuals through space and time and you have one of the highest-quality data sources in marine biology.

OSEA builds the technical infrastructure that makes this practical at scale: detection models, identification algorithms, citizen-science submission tools, partner-organization onboarding, and the open catalogues that result.

§ II How we work

Open by default. Approved photographs and identification records are published openly, with submitter consent and with locations scrubbed against poaching. Datasets are exported in standard formats (Darwin Core) on request.

Partner-friendly. Aquariums, dive shops, schools, research labs, anyone who can drive submissions or co-host a program, gets a free partner account, branded landing page, and dedicated QR codes.

No vendor lock-in. The platform is open source. Models are pretrained on publicly available wildlife re-ID weights (MiewID, Wild Me) and fine-tuned on our species-specific data.

§ III Where we are

Headquartered on the California coast. Field activities currently centred on the San Francisco Bay and outer-coast California. Programs are open to expansion in any temperate or tropical coastal region with partner support.
