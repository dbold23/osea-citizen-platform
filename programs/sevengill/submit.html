Submissions are paused

The sighting form is temporarily closed while we finish the system that receives and stores submissions. Please don't send photographs yet.

We'd rather turn people away for a few weeks than accept a photograph we can't yet promise to keep. The catalogue will reopen once submissions are being recorded properly and we can stand behind everything in our data & privacy policy.

If you photographed a broadnose sevengill in the meantime, please hold on to the original file. Keeping the untouched original preserves the date, location, and camera details we need, so nothing is lost by waiting.

Thank you for wanting to contribute. Please check back before sending a sighting.
