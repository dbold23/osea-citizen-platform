Support the people who do the work.

OSEA is small, independent, and runs on the time of student researchers, volunteer divers, and a handful of paid staff. Donations support those people and the operations that keep the program afloat, fieldwork, partner outreach, training, and the unglamorous details of running a nonprofit.

Each tier opens a Stripe-hosted checkout in a new tab. Recurring options are available there. OSEA Science is organized as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit; donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. See § III below for the per-tier benefit disclosure required by IRS rules.

§ I Where the money goes

OSEA publishes its allocation each year. The targets below reflect our operating budget, not a wishlist.

42%

People

Student stipends, research assistantships, and modest staff salaries. The platform doesn't run without them.

26%

Fieldwork

Boat charters, dive equipment, fuel, weather days. The unglamorous side of marine science.

15%

Volunteers & training

Free workshops, citizen-scientist onboarding, school programs, and partner-organization training.

10%

Operations

Insurance, registration, accounting, hosting, equipment depreciation. The costs no one wants to underwrite, that we still have to cover.

7%

Open data & outreach

Conference travel, peer-reviewed publication fees, public-data archiving, and the occasional poster.

No portion of donations goes to fundraising consultants, marketing agencies, or executive bonuses. We don't have any. Our annual financial report is published openly each January.

§ II Other ways to help

Submit a sighting. Photographs are the catalogue. A clean profile of a sevengill is worth hours of fieldwork.

Register your organization. Aquariums, dive shops, schools, research labs, anyone who can drive submissions or co-host a program, gets a free partner page in their name.

Bequests & planned giving. If you want to support OSEA in your estate plan, write to example@oseascience.com and we'll connect you with our advisor.

Volunteer in the field. Each summer we run training cohorts for new field volunteers. Watch the main OSEA site for openings.

A note on recognition

Donor names are listed in the colophon and on the contributor wall only with explicit consent. Anonymous giving is the default for those who prefer it, and just as appreciated.

§ III Tax & tier-benefit disclosure

OSEA Science (Ocean Science Expedition Academy) is organized as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Please consult your tax advisor for guidance on your specific situation.

Quid pro quo notice. Where a donation tier includes tangible goods or services (a printed Field Card, signed print, mailed sticker, or in-person lab tour), only the portion of your contribution exceeding the fair market value of those benefits is tax-deductible. Estimated good-faith fair market values per tier:

  • Subscriber ($25): $3 (sticker), deductible portion ~$22
  • Naturalist ($75): $12 (sticker, printed Field Card), deductible portion ~$63
  • Patron ($250): $35 (above plus signed print, adoption certificate), deductible portion ~$215
  • Society ($1,000+): $75 (above plus printed Society Volume), deductible portion is your contribution minus $75

For donations of $250 or more, OSEA will provide a written contemporaneous acknowledgment by January 31 of the following year, itemizing your contribution and the fair market value of any benefits received, as required by IRS regulations.

If you'd like to refuse the tier benefits and maximize the deductible portion of your gift, mention this when you donate (or write to us afterward) and we'll log the gift as benefit-free, making the full amount deductible.

EIN, board roster, and most recent IRS Form 990 are available on request and will be linked here once published. Recognition of 501(c)(3) status takes time; if our IRS recognition status changes, this page will be updated.
