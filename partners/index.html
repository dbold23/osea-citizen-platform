Partners

Organizations doing the work.

Aquariums, dive shops, schools, and research labs drive most submissions. Each gets their own landing page, branded QR codes, and a free seat at the moderator table.

§ II What partners get

A branded landing page. sightings.oseascience.com/partners/your-organization/ — with your logo, your copy, and a submission form pre-tagged so we can see how many sightings come from your community.

Printable QR codes. Posters, postcards, exhibit placards. Drop one at the dive shop counter and your visitors can submit straight from their phone.

A moderator account (optional). If your organization has someone qualified to triage submissions, we'll give them moderator access to the queue.

Quarterly impact reports. A short PDF showing how many submissions, how many new individuals, and how many cross-time resightings came through your channel last quarter.
